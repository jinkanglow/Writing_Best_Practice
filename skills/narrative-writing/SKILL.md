# Narrative Writing

Track character knowledge, motivation, decision, action, cost and changed state. Preserve intentional repetition and viewpoint shifts. Prefer specific observable actions when they carry meaning, but do not ban direct emotion or exposition categorically. Scene order and beat percentages are genre-specific optional constraints, not universal laws.
