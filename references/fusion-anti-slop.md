# Fusion-Specific Anti-Slop Review

## Purpose

Preserve a primary story's structural and emotional obligations while removing generic language patterns **without flattening the voice or deleting necessary beats**.

## Review grid

| Level | Question | Evidence required | Typical intervention | False-positive safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lexical | Does a phrase substitute for observation? | Exact span and its intended meaning | Replace with situation-specific wording | Preserve idiom in dialogue, quotations and purposeful register |
| Sentence | Is the claim a conclusion without a scene? | Sentence and preceding action | Show consequence or remove redundant summary | Concise telling can bridge scenes |
| Paragraph | Does it add a distinct change? | Before/after state and scene function | Add consequence or compress | Atmosphere and pacing may be legitimate functions |
| Structural | Are emotional beats rendered identically? | Cross-scene gesture inventory | Vary the causal choice, not merely synonyms | Repeated behavior may be characterization |
| Reasoning | Is psychology asserted without prior setup? | Earlier established habit and present trigger | Plant setup, revise decision | Limited point of view can intentionally withhold motive |
| Information | What does the reader learn or newly infer? | New fact, choice, cost, relationship or contradiction | Replace vague significance with concrete consequence | Ambiguity may be the intended effect |

## Non-negotiable review rules

1. Never delete a primary anchor solely because its current wording is clichéd. Repair its **expression**.
2. Never convert all emotional narration to body-language clichés. “She bit her lip” is not more truthful than well-placed interior narration.
3. Never require a mundane object after every emotional peak. Grounding works only when the object belongs to the established world.
4. Never impose a universal zero-em-dash or zero-adverb rule on all genres and languages.
5. Do not assert a scene is unique solely because it contains a named object. Its significance must follow from history, constraint, or consequence.
6. After any local rewrite, rerun the anchor map and compare character decisions; a stylistic improvement that removes causal function is a regression.

## Review record

For each finding: `location`, `level`, `quoted_span`, `why_generic`, `primary_anchor_at_risk`, `proposed_revision`, `exception_or_counterexample`, `reviewer_decision`. If no anchor is affected, say so. If the issue cannot be judged without the full manuscript, mark `HUMAN_REVIEW`.
