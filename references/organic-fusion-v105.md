# Organic skill fusion: operational style

Trigger on 融合这两个技能 / 把X规范应用到Y故事里 / 以X为主框架套用Y的语言标准 / skill融合. The first skill owns content and structure; subsequent documents or personal habit lists constrain expression. Deliver the complete fused article or story, not the internal review ledger.

## Decompose before drafting
Record mandatory events or claims, relationship logic or causal mechanism, emotional curve or argument progression, and indispensable sections. Separately record banned expressions, sentence patterns, concrete detail standards and information density. For each conflict, preserve the primary anchor and change its expression. If no workable mapping exists, return to translation. Character plausibility and factual accuracy outrank style preferences. A necessary direct line of dialogue is allowed if it belongs to that character.

## Translate
A high point becomes a small departure from established behavior (a towel laid out without comment). Fear becomes a recurring reaction or remembered incident. Growing closeness can be a touch withdrawn or a note left behind. A rupture may arise from control meeting avoidance; establish those habits before the rupture. Growth is shown through a reversed choice, not a declaration. In nonfiction, map each anchor to a sourced fact, calculation, causal link or distinct interpretation. Never invent a scene in reporting.

## Transition review
At warmth-to-distance, approach-to-withdrawal, conflict-to-compromise, trust-to-doubt or desire-to-fear, ask: does this passage change structure, does it belong to these characters, and does the expression earn its emotional effect? A removable passage may still supply rhythm or atmosphere. A transferable detail is a review signal, not automatic failure. Direct interiority may be appropriate. Record a reason for each decision. Scan 真正/确实/不得不说/值得深思/标志着 and 他感到/她意识到/他明白; revise unnecessary instances without deleting evidence or corrupting dialogue. Ordinary detail after a peak can ground emotion when it fits the scene; do not insert arbitrary objects.

## Closure
For the optional romance profile, review first contact and unusual gesture at 0–10%, three varied small peaks at 10–60%, interrupted near-kiss at 45–60%, defensive rupture at 70–75%, empty routine at 75–85%, changed action at 85–100%. These are chosen planning targets, not universal narrative laws. Check character-driven darkness and fear-linked peaks. Review repeated reactions across scenes, generic summaries, false contrasts and unnecessary exposition. A flat ending may need a concrete return to an earlier image, not a moral. If structure reads like an outline, remap anchors to action; if clean prose feels empty, restore missing emotional anchors; if behavior is implausible, establish motivation earlier. Split unresolved conflicts into smaller expressive decisions.

For financial analysis, retain figures, periods, units, source attribution and material qualifications. Consolidate repeated defensive disclaimers; each section adds evidence, mechanism or decision relevance. No unsupported certainty, invented reporting or claim of AI-detector avoidance.
