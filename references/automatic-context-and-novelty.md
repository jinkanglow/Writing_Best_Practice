# Context extraction and evidence-grounded novelty

Extract exact spans for time, units, conditions and relation markers. Treat these as candidates, not complete semantic frames. Entity and metric resolution remain editorial tasks; never fill missing context by guessing.

For every new claim, link an exact quotation to the source snapshot and its SHA-256. Distinguish a new quotation from a new inference: the same source may support a genuinely new calculation, and a different quotation may repeat an existing argument. Review the method and conclusion, not merely the count of new quotations.

When a qualifier disappears, inspect its effect on scope, causality, uncertainty and numerical meaning. Preserve material limits in the revised prose; consolidate only redundant disclaimers. A bound reviewer must explain why meaning survives. Do not automatically insert or delete qualifiers.

Example: “Sales rose 10% excluding returns” cannot become “Sales rose 10%” without evidence that the exclusion is immaterial. “Delivery time fell” does not independently establish weaker demand. State the measurement once, then explain the causal alternatives and what would distinguish them.

Release requires the existing context and orchestration gates plus evidence and coverage review. A ready result is not permission to publish. Benchmark real cases separately from synthetic fixtures; retain provenance, permission, labels and input hashes. No independent blind review is implied by execution.
