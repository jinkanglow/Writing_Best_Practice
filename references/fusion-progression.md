# Fusion progression and causal review

Normalize each skill directive into a target, scope and require/forbid action. Compare opposing directives in overlapping scopes before drafting. This detects explicit registered conflicts, not every natural-language contradiction. Resolve by preserving the primary anchor and changing its expression; integrity cannot be waived.

For fiction, record the earlier trigger and later payoff as exact spans. Edges must point forward in scene order. A graph establishes traceability, not psychological plausibility. A reviewer checks motive, alternatives, emotional continuity and whether the payoff is earned. Parallel scenes may require a documented relationship rather than a direct causal link.

For nonfiction, register each unit's contribution as new evidence, new mechanism, calculation or necessary recap. Review in document order. A new claim ID is not proof of novelty; compare proposition, scope, source and inference. A recap must serve a specific reader need. Keep material qualifications; consolidate repeated defensive statements without weakening the evidence boundary.

For style exceptions, compare the exact draft wording with a concrete alternative. Explain why the alternative harms meaning or voice. Never exempt accuracy or attribution. Review decisions bind to the input hash; changes require renewed review.

Use `node scripts/editorial-v107.js audit examples/v107/input.json`. Exit 3 means incomplete review, 4 means blocked, 2 means invalid input. Verify by recomputation. Ready means ready for independent review, not permission to publish. Benchmark cases must be labeled synthetic or real; declared provenance is not authenticated. No real independent ratings are supplied.
