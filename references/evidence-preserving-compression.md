# Evidence-preserving compression

Assign each section a distinct analytical question. Map each material proposition to its exact text, scope, polarity, modality and evidence references. Preserve dates, units, denominators, attribution and causal direction. Merge repeated propositions only when their scope and meaning agree. A new mechanism, counterexample or independent observation is not redundant merely because it shares a conclusion.

Use the compression audit to locate lexical equivalence candidates (token Jaccard >= 0.65, an uncalibrated review threshold), count new declared propositions by section in supplied reading order, and flag repeated uncertainty formulas. The counts are not semantic information gain. Review paraphrases and contradictions manually, including those with little lexical overlap. Consolidate repeated caveats where they affect the conclusion; never remove a material uncertainty to make prose more decisive.

Before release, independently review every mapped claim and check the full text for omitted or newly introduced claims. Evidence identifiers are not proof that evidence is authentic. Numeric literal checks are conservative and do not verify units or arithmetic equivalence. Run the existing orchestration gate separately. Deliver the complete fused article, not the audit, unless requested.
