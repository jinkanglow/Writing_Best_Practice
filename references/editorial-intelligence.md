# Argument identity, source lineage and author contribution

Read before rewriting research-based nonfiction. Preserve the author's thesis and necessary evidence; do not expand a research summary merely by changing its wording.

## Semantic argument review
Assign each material claim a canonical proposition and scope (entity, period, metric and conditions). Map it to an exact passage. Two passages with the same proposition and scope are candidates for consolidation, even if their wording differs. Keep a recurrence when it serves a distinct purpose, such as a conclusion that resolves a previously introduced question. Do not merge opposite claims or different periods. The tool compares editor-supplied canonical labels; it does not infer semantic equivalence. Review all unmapped material, including headings and tables outside the scanner's coverage.

## Source dependency graph
Record the source text, locator and upstream source IDs. Two reports quoting one research note have one declared root, not two independent confirmations. Trace claims to the actual evidence and check dates, definitions and source permissions. A graph cannot establish that its declared lineage is true. Never invent a primary source to fill a gap.

## Original contribution ledger
Record the author's own calculation, reporting, comparison, interpretation or synthesis, its method, result, artifact locator and dependent claims. A paraphrase alone is not an independent contribution. For a calculation retain inputs, units and arithmetic; for reporting retain interview or observation records; for interpretation state the mechanism and disconfirming evidence. Do not label AI-generated analysis as firsthand reporting. The ledger validates records, not the underlying artifacts or originality.

## Release review
Run the integrated audit, resolve each finding by revision or a documented reason to retain it, and re-run after every change. A reviewer records the review basis, identity, author, publisher-policy evidence, decision and per-finding dispositions. Distinct names are not authenticated independence. A ready result is only eligibility for release review; run existing factual and craft gates separately. Respect publisher AI-use and disclosure rules. Deliver the fused article, not the internal ledger, unless the user asks for it.
