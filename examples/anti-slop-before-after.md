# Context-aware edits (all examples invented)

## Empty significance
Before: This groundbreaking initiative will revolutionize the landscape.
After: The new checkout flow removes the manual approval step; the team has not yet measured whether it reduces abandoned orders.
Why: identifies the changed process and leaves the result unverified.

## Do not manufacture specificity
Before: Our users love the product.
Wrong edit: 34 users returned the next day.
Better edit: We do not yet have retention data; the current claim should be removed or supported by analytics.

## Fiction: showing versus telling
Before: She realized he was frightened.
Alternative: He checked the door twice, then left his keys in the lock.
Exception: Direct interior narration is valid when consistent with point of view and scene purpose.

## Technical false positive
Before: We ran robust regression with clustered standard errors.
Decision: Retain `robust`; it is technical terminology, not empty promotion.

## Skill fusion
Anchor: protagonist chooses to trust someone despite a history of avoidance.
Constraint: no generic declarations of growth.
Translation: she hands over the original letter rather than a photocopy, then waits while it is read.
Check: the action must have an established cost in earlier scenes.
